Concurrency Control

Atomic Slot Locking

When a patient is reviewing a proposed time, the system writes an expiring hold timestamp into the database, blocking other inbound patients from claiming that exact doctor window.

Doctor Scoping

Specialty Grounding

Appointments are strictly scoped to verified doctor catalogs per clinic branch. The LLM cannot book a pediatric patient with an orthopedic surgeon or book outside branch operating hours.

Automated Reminders

24h / 2h Interactive Alerts

Cron queues trigger personalized reminder messages with interactive WhatsApp buttons (Confirm / Reschedule / Cancel). Confirmations immediately update calendar status flags.

Human Takeover

1-Click Live Takeover

Doctors or medical receptionists can toggle the chat pause switch in the web dashboard, immediately suspending the AI and preventing conflicting simultaneous automated responses.
